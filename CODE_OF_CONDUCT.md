# Code of conduct

Unit Converter welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Help keep project spaces respectful, useful, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and follow reasonable moderation requests.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
personal attacks, sharing someone's private information without permission,
and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
community spaces maintained for the project.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at
[support@jiripolasek.com](mailto:support@jiripolasek.com?subject=Unit%20Converter%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20Report).
Include relevant links and context. Do not post private report details in
public issues or discussions.

Maintainers will review the context and choose a proportionate response.
They may ask someone to change their behavior, remove inappropriate content,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation in project spaces.
Report details should be shared only as needed to address the concern.
